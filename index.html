03 • COSMETIC PROBLEMS

Real concerns. Better solutions.

Common cosmetic concerns that may affect the customer experience:

01

Skin Irritation

Drawback: Some customers with sensitive skin may experience irritation or redness after using certain products.

02

Foundation Shade Selection

Drawback: Customers may find it difficult to choose the right foundation shade because there are many shades and undertones.

03

Complex Ingredient Information

Drawback: Ingredient lists may contain many technical names that are difficult for everyday customers to understand.

04

Limited Product Reviews

Drawback: Some official product pages may show “Reviews (0)”, giving customers less information from other buyers' experiences.

05

Fragrance Concern

Drawback: Some products list fragrance in their ingredients, which may be a concern for customers who are sensitive to fragrance.

04 • PROCESS SOLUTION

Step by step towards a better beauty experience.

Each customer concern is translated into a clear and practical solution process.

01

Skin Compatibility Testing

Process solution: Conduct skin compatibility testing and provide clear patch-test guidance.

02

Shade Identification

Process solution: Provide a shade guide and a process for identifying skin tone and undertone.

03

Ingredient Explanation

Process solution: Explain ingredient functions using simple, customer-friendly language.

04

Customer Feedback

Process solution: Encourage customers to leave ratings, comments and photos after purchase.

05

Clear Ingredient Warning

Process solution: Display ingredient labels and warnings more clearly.

07 • SOBELLA PRODUCT BENEFITS

Beauty products with purpose.

SOBELLA products are designed to support a practical beauty routine and give customers a more comfortable makeup experience.

01 SOBELLA Hydration Booster Moisturizer Helps hydrate the skin, leaving it softer and smoother, helping makeup look more even and reducing patchy makeup caused by dry skin.
02 SOBELLA Brightening Primer Helps the skin feel smoother, provides a natural brightening effect and helps foundation apply more evenly.
